## Note-Taking Approach

My notes focus on:
- Key concepts and methodologies
- Critical insights and "aha" moments
- Questions and areas for further exploration
- Connections between topics
- Practical applications

NB: with the rise of AI, I have used it to help me structure my messy notes sometimes, what I would do is that I screenshot my notes, ask the AI to structure and give examples to help me better reconnect and understand/grasp the concepts better then I would go and try to write and structure my thoughts into my notes.
